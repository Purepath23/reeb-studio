Privacy Policy – Pump Up the Volume!

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Developer: REEB Studio – Software Development & AI Solutions

Pump Up the Volume! ("the app") does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data.

What the app does on your device

  • It applies an audio effect to the sound played by your device so that it is louder.
  • It saves a few settings on your device only: whether the boost is on, the boost level you chose, when your free trial started, and whether you have unlocked the app.
  • It shows a notification while the boost is on.
  • If you allow it, it reads how loud the sound your phone is playing is, only while the app is open, to move the level meter. This is the only reason it asks for the “Record audio” permission, and the permission is optional.

Purchases

The app offers a free trial and a one-time purchase to unlock it. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. The app does not receive or store your payment details; it only learns from Google Play whether your Google account has unlocked the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send any data over the internet: the app has no accounts, no servers and no analytics of its own.
  • It does not use advertising or tracking. The only outside component is Google's own Play Billing Library, used to handle the purchase.
  • It does not use the microphone and does not record, store or send any audio. The level meter only reads how loud the sound is, shows it on the screen, and never saves or shares it.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, photos or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
