What it does

One tap

One big button turns the boost on or off, one slider chooses how much louder. Changes apply instantly while you listen.

Live level meter

A digital LED meter follows your music and visibly jumps up when the boost is on. It is optional and never records audio.

Simple pricing

Free for 3 days, then one payment unlocks it for good. No subscription, no ads, no tracking.
